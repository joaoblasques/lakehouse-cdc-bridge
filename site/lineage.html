The query behind it

The same walk in SQL, on Databricks or locally:

SELECT a.rule, a.account_id, b.op, b.source_position, b.source_commit_ts,
       au.topic, au.kafka_partition, au.kafka_offset, au.payload_hash
FROM   gold_fraud_alerts a
JOIN   bronze_change_events b
       ON b.source_table = 'dbo.card_transactions'
      AND get_json_object(b.key_json, '$.tx_id') IN (SELECT explode(a.evidence_keys))
JOIN   cdc_event_audit au ON au.event_id = b.event_id
WHERE  a.alert_id = :alert_id
ORDER  BY b.source_sequence;
